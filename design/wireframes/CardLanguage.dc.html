The card language — 16 named patterns

Every kind of thing the consultant says has exactly one shape it renders as. Pattern name → example with real copy → when it fires. This is the content layer the design system was missing: tokens + components below it, screens above it.

1 · THE READ
People arrive at your checkout already sold — your brand does that work. I found seven moments where some of those sales slip away. 7 moments 2 in full
Opens every report. Strength first, then recoverable sales, then count chips. Never a score, never an indictment.
2 · THE STEELMAN OPENER
Charging for return coverage is a legitimate call — apparel returns run 20–30% and somebody pays for them. The problem isn't the fee. It's that your product page already promised it free.
First block of every full finding. Grants the strongest honest reason the design is intentional, then names where the trade-off leaks. The adversary stage computes it; the card surfaces it.
3 · THE RINGED EXHIBIT
Subtotal$40.00 Checkout+ ⓘ$2.98 your cart drawer · captured 9:41am
Their pixels, one dashed ring, a capture timestamp. The default proof form — a claim without one is a held row, not a finding.
4 · THE COUNTING EXHIBIT
Only 2 left! Only 2 left! +12
When the evidence is repetition: two real instances + the counter chip. “14 of 16” lands harder shown than said.
5 · THE CONTRADICTION SPLIT
YOUR HERO
Free returns, always. YOUR CART
Final sale. Unlock free returns — $2.98.
Two of their own statements, side by side, labeled by location. No commentary needed — the layout is the argument.
6 · THE COMPETITOR ROW
WHAT YOUR CUSTOMER COMPARES YOU TO
Bonobos' banner, checked this morning: “Free Shipping & Returns in the US.” One sentence, no asterisk.
Only when verified this run AND actually contrasting (voice law V8). A competitor doing the same thing kills the row — research cites instead.
7 · THE RESEARCH LINE
Unexpected costs at checkout are the most-cited reason carts die — 48% of abandoning shoppers name them (Baymard, 2024).
One stat, one source, linked. Always from the knowledge base — a percentage with no source fails the build (V7).
8 · THE FIX PAIR
A · The patch — “+ $14.90 flat shipping” beside every price. no dev · today B · The proper fix — running total in the cart drawer. ≈2 dev-days
Always a pair: the patch that ships today and the proper fix with its cost. One option reads as an order; two read as advice.
9 · THE BEFORE / AFTER
NOW
$184.00 AFTER A
$184.00
+ $14.90 flat shipping
Their element, redrawn in their layout with our copy. The single most persuasive artifact — used on the floor finding and in every paid fix.
10 · THE COST CHIP
copy · 20 min · you theme toggle · 10 min ≈1 dev-day · checkout template
Who · effort range · dependency. Ranges, never false precision; re-priced by the intake answers. Appears on every option and roadmap item.
11 · THE HELD ROW
Mid-purchase, your size guide opens a PDF on top of the checkout.
One located sentence + the lock. Specific enough to verify, incomplete enough to want. Never vague, never blurred text.
12 · THE JOURNEY STRIP
9Product 🔒4Cart 🔒6Checkout 🔒5Pay
Real counts, locked contents, one teaser sentence underneath (“the loudest thing isn't on this page”). The scale-shower.
13 · THE SPOT-IT
FINAL SALE One of the held nine is in this crop. See it? I give up — show me
One per report. A clear crop, ring hidden — the reader hunts, invests, and feels the expertise gap either way. The give-up reveal doubles as the accessibility path.
14 · VERIFICATION & REGRESSION
✓ Verified. Re-checked overnight — the patch shows beside every price. ⚠ Regressed. Thursday's theme update reverted it. [Reopen] [That was on purpose]
The retention spine. Dated, in writing, with an escape hatch — a fix made differently than A or B verifies as “fixed, your way.”
15 · THE WALKED-CLEAN PASS
I walked this page twice looking for trouble. It holds up. One thing worth a look — your reviews sit below three screens of specs — and that's the list.
The zero-findings case. Short, honest, specific about the one thing. Credibility banked for the next walk — and the most screenshotted card we'll ship.
16 · THE DECISION BRIEF
Checkout: 4 steps → 2. A: re-order fields, ≈2 dev-days · B: one-page app, ≈$600 + setup. Recommendation: A — your template supports it and holiday code freeze favors fewer moving parts. Or park it until Dec 26 — a legitimate third door.
For structural items only. Options, costs, a recommendation with its reason, and “park it” as a first-class choice. The most consultant-grade artifact in the product.

Screen-level patterns (the intake conversation, the roadmap phase card, the habits grid) compose from these. Rule of composition: a full finding = 2 → 3/4/5 → 6 (if verified) → 7 → 8 (+9) → 10. A held finding = 11 alone. Nothing renders in a shape that isn't on this sheet.
